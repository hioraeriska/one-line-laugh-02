# One-Line Laugh

One funny line for [The One-Line Laugh](https://make.fun/bounty/u-ronigrv-the-one-line-laugh-7ccca46f) on make.fun: a single sentence of at most 200 characters, judged pairwise by a house judge weighing a real surprise at the end, an everyday truth, and economy of words.

`main.py` validates the line against the bounty gate (one line, 1–200 characters, exactly one sentence) and writes it to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`.

The line: *"I went to bed early for once, and my brain treated it as a meeting to discuss every mistake I've made since 2009."* (113 characters)
